## The idea

- What it is: Performance-vetted recruitment wedge and operational assessment engine for Supply Chain & Operations talent.
- Who it is for: Plant Directors, Operations Managers, and Supply Chain Directors at manufacturing and logistics companies (structured Italian SMEs and mid-sized industrial enterprises in Lombardy, Emilia-Romagna, and Veneto) seeking junior/entry-level operations talent without wrong-hire risks.
- What it sells, at what price: 3 pre-vetted junior/graduate candidates proven through an interactive 25-minute operational simulation of factory bottlenecks and inventory trade-offs, sold on a 100% "Guarantee-per-Hire" success fee of 12% to 15% of first-year gross annual salary (RAL) (~€3,600 - €4,500 for a €30,000 RAL), invoiced at 90 days only if the candidate successfully completes their probation period.
- Where and how: Online assessment platform with founder-led boutique placement service across Northern Italy. Candidates acquired via direct university partnerships (PoliMi, PoliTo, PoliBa, UNIBO) and ITS Logistics academies. B2B clients reached via direct founder outbound on LinkedIn to Plant & Operations Directors.
- Budget and constraints: Bootstrapped / pre-product by a Management Engineering founder; €0 paid acquisition budget; 3-week pragmatic build for the simulation engine; zero upfront software licensing fee for clients (pure contingency recruitment wedge).

### 1. Top Risks Raised by Multiple Members

1. **Cash Flow Lag from 90-Day Guarantee (Offers & Monopoly):**
   Invoicing exclusively after 90 days of successful probation creates a 120- to 150-day gap between founder effort and actual cash collection. A string of placements in months 1-3 could cause working capital exhaustion before revenues arrive.
2. **Candidate Acquisition & Assessment Dropout (Offers & Product):**
   High-caliber engineering graduates (PoliMi, PoliTo) hold multiple job options. If the 25-minute simulation is framed as an unpaid pre-employment hoop rather than a VIP fast-track directly to executive decision-makers, completion rates will crater.
3. **Cheating & Simulation Integrity via LLMs (Product & Monopoly):**
   Candidates copying data tables into ChatGPT/Claude could easily pass the quantitative stockout test and generate synthetic rationale, eroding client trust in the scorecard if not hardened with dynamic parameters.
4. **Disintermediation & Circumvention Risk (Offers & Monopoly):**
   Italian manufacturing SMEs may attempt to interview or hire introduced candidates without paying the placement fee unless legal terms are signed prior to candidate scorecard unmasking.

## Pricing

  - Traditional agencies (Page Personnel, Hays, Wyser) charge 15%–20% (€4,500 – €6,000) payable on contract signing with weak replacement guarantees.

- **Unit Margin:**
  - Variable cost per placement: €200 (LLM grader tokens, academic credential check, contracting).
  - Contribution margin per placement: **€4,000 (95.2%)**.
  - At planned volume (3 placements/month), net operational profit margin is **74%**.
