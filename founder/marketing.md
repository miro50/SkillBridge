# Go-To-Market & Marketing Plan — SkillBridge

## 1. Evidence Synthesis

- **Primary Audience:** Managing Directors and Plant Directors of mid-sized industrial manufacturing companies (50–250 employees) in Northern Italy (Lombardy, Emilia-Romagna, Veneto). In the buyer panel, MDs/Owners converted at **70%** and Operations Managers at **60%**, primarily driven by immediate hiring urgency and disgust with keyword-matched agency CVs.
- **Competitor Differentiation:** Traditional agencies (Page Personnel, Hays) charge 15–20% on day 1 with generic CVs; testing SaaS (TestGorilla) forces expensive annual software lock-ins without providing candidates.
- **Financial Envelope (CAC Cap):** Unit contribution margin is **€3,940**. Even with a conservative 25% allocation to customer acquisition, the maximum allowable CAC is **€985 per placement**. With direct founder LinkedIn outbound, estimated CAC is **<€150**.

## 2. Positioning Statement

### Three Positioning Options:
1. *The Risk-Reversal Angle:* "For Plant Directors frustrated by wrong-hires, SkillBridge is the only operations recruitment service that bills 100% after the 90-day probation period, unlike traditional agencies that demand thousands upfront."
2. *The Practical Engineering Angle (RECOMMENDED):* 
   > **"For Plant & Operations Directors in Italian manufacturing who are exhausted by theoretical CVs that can't handle a shop-floor crisis, SkillBridge is the performance-vetted recruitment clearinghouse that delivers graduate engineers proven on a 25-minute assembly line breakdown simulation, unlike traditional headhunters who filter passive buzzwords."**
3. *The Speed & Wedge Angle:* "For fast-growing manufacturing plants needing immediate planning capacity, SkillBridge delivers 3 ready-to-interview operations specialists in under 7 days with proven math scorecards."

*Panel Support:* Option 2 directly activates the #1 buying trigger cited by 84% of Urgent Problem Solvers and 60% of Agency Skeptics: *"Finalmente qualcuno che mi fa vedere i numeri del test prima di farmi perdere tempo"* (P002).

## 3. Channels Strategy

### Active Channels to Launch:
1. **Targeted Founder-Led LinkedIn Outbound (B2B Clients — 70% of effort):**
   - *Why:* Plant Directors and Supply Chain Managers in Italian manufacturing (Bergamo, Brescia, Bologna, Modena, Vicenza) are easily identifiable and accessible on LinkedIn.
   - *Method:* Highly personalized, non-salesy messages presenting 1 anonymous high-scoring candidate scorecard and offering a zero-cost 20-minute interview.
   - *Cost:* €150/mo (Sales Navigator).
2. **University Department & Student Engineering Association Partnerships (Candidate Pipeline — 25% of effort):**
   - *Why:* Management Engineering graduate students congregate around departmental associations (e.g. JEME PoliMi, BEST Milano, JEToP Torino) and career days.
   - *Method:* Position the simulation as the *"Plant Fast-Track Pass"*: skip HR screening and get a direct interview with a Plant Director.
   - *Cost:* €500 one-off sponsorship + networking coffee meetings.
3. **Regional Industrial Associations / Confindustria Gruppi Meccatronica (5% of effort):**
   - *Why:* Unmatched B2B trust anchor in Italian manufacturing clusters.
   - *Method:* Guest technical article on *"L'asimmetria informativa nel recruiting tecnico industriale"* or local breakfast seminar.

### What We Are NOT Doing (And Why):
- **NO Meta / Google Paid Ads:** B2B Plant Directors do not click Facebook ads for recruitment; paid search CPC for "recruitment agency" is inflated (€8–€15/click) by staffing conglomerates.
- **NO Generic Job Board Scraping:** Avoids commoditizing candidate relationships.

## 4. The 10 High-Converting Hooks

Each hook directly dismantles one verified buyer objection or amplifies a top buying reason:

1. **The Downtime Cost Hook:** *"Una linea di montaggio ferma costa 1.200 € all'ora. Il tuo ultimo assunto in pianificazione sapeva calcolare il punto di riordino prima del colloquio?"* (Answers objection: Why practical testing matters).
2. **The Agency Burn Hook:** *"Basta pagare il 18% di RAL ad agenzie che vi mandano CV con la parola 'Lean' cercata con Ctrl+F."* (Answers objection: Recruiter incompetence).
3. **The Anti-Cheat Hook:** *"Perché il nostro test di 25 minuti non si può barare con ChatGPT: variabili dinamiche, blocco appunti e audit matematico deterministico."* (Neutralizes Trust/AI objection).
4. **The Zero-Risk Hook:** *"Tre neolaureati in Ingegneria Gestionale per la tua Supply Chain. Zero costi di attivazione, fattura a 90 giorni solo se superano il periodo di prova."* (Answers Risk/Cash objection).
5. **The Speed Hook:** *"Dalla vacancy aperta a 3 candidati pre-validati sulla tua scrivania in 7 giorni lavorativi."* (Answers Urgent Need objection).
6. **The Retention Warranty Hook:** *"E se il candidato dopo 4 mesi se ne va a Milano? La nostra garanzia di sostituzione gratuita copre 180 giorni completi."* (Neutralizes Resignation fear).
7. **The Free Interview Hook:** *"Fai 20 minuti di colloquio conoscitivo a costo zero: se non ti piacciono, amici come prima e non spendi un centesimo."* (Answers Commitment anxiety).
8. **The Practical vs Theory Hook:** *"Tutti sanno definire il 'Kanban' a voce. Quanti sanno salvare una commessa quando il fornitore triplica il lead time?"* (Answers Theory vs Practice objection).
9. **The No-HR-Lockin Hook:** *"Nessun contratto di fornitura vincolante o esclusiva pluriennale: risolviamo la singola vacancy aperta adesso."* (Neutralizes Habit/Corporate vendor objection).
10. **The Scarcity Hook:** *"5 posti pilota disponibili per il distretto meccatronico lombardo/emiliano a fee ridotta dell'11%."* (Drives launch action).

## 6. Marketing Budget & Weekly KPI Dashboard

### Budget Allocation (Month 1 — €800 Total):
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator & InMail credits: €150
- University student club sponsorship / campus promotions: €500
- Software warmup / outbound email domains: €150

### Three Numbers to Watch Weekly:
1. **Outreach-to-Interview Rate:** % of contacted Plant Directors with open roles who accept a 20-min candidate interview (Target: **>20%**; if <10%, rewrite hook).
2. **Simulation Completion Rate:** % of invited candidates who finish the 25-min assessment (Target: **>70%**; if <50%, shorten briefing).
3. **Interview-to-Offer Rate:** % of candidates interviewed by clients who receive a job offer (Target: **>50%**; if <30%, calibrate simulation difficulty).
