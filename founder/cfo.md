# Unit economics: SkillBridge (Operational Assessment & Recruitment for Supply Chain)

## The CFO's Executive Note

- **The Margin:** At the planned volume of 3 successful placements per month, the business delivers a **72% net operating margin** after all operating overhead and founder stipend. Every placed candidate generates **$3,940 (94%)** in contribution margin.
- **The Line to Watch — The 90-Day Cash Realization Gap:** While accounting break-even is low at **1 placement per month**, payments under the 100% guarantee model are received 90 days after start date (plus typical 30-day corporate payment cycles). Therefore, cash from Month 1 hires arrives in Month 4. The primary operational risk is working capital exhaustion during the first 90–120 days.
- **Cash Needed to Start:** The direct startup spend is **$1,800** (legal contract drafting, domain, campus marketing). However, to fund 3 months of fixed overhead ($2,700/mo) before the first placement check clears, the founder needs a **$9,900 liquidity runway** ($1,800 startup spend + $8,100 working capital buffer).
- **Three Ways to Improve the Numbers:**
  1. *Staged Invoicing (30% on start / 70% at 90 days):* Invoicing $1,260 upon contract signature eliminates the working capital hole and reduces required starting capital to $1,800.
  2. *Retainer Duo Packaging:* Selling 2 hires upfront for $7,500 with a $2,500 commitment deposit.
  3. *Price Optimization post-pilot:* Elevating the standard fee from 14% to 16% RAL ($4,800) once 5 case studies are in hand boosts Year 1 profit to $126,500 (+20%).
- **Board Financial Condition Status:** MET WITH REQUIREMENT. The board's warning on cash realization lag is completely verified. The founder must maintain a €10,000 cash/overdraft cushion or mandate a 30% start deposit on pilot placements.

*Note: All figures are pre-tax. An Italian commercialista must audit tax structure (s.r.l. vs regime forfettario), electronic SDI invoicing, and statutory employee/contractor contributions before launching.*
