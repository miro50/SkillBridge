## 1. Top Risks Raised by Multiple Members

1. **Cash Flow Lag from 90-Day Guarantee (Offers & Monopoly):**
   Invoicing exclusively after 90 days of successful probation creates a 120- to 150-day gap between founder effort and actual cash collection. A string of placements in months 1-3 could cause working capital exhaustion before revenues arrive.
2. **Candidate Acquisition & Assessment Dropout (Offers & Product):**
   High-caliber engineering graduates (PoliMi, PoliTo) hold multiple job options. If the 25-minute simulation is framed as an unpaid pre-employment hoop rather than a VIP fast-track directly to executive decision-makers, completion rates will crater.
3. **Cheating & Simulation Integrity via LLMs (Product & Monopoly):**
   Candidates copying data tables into ChatGPT/Claude could easily pass the quantitative stockout test and generate synthetic rationale, eroding client trust in the scorecard if not hardened with dynamic parameters.
4. **Disintermediation & Circumvention Risk (Offers & Monopoly):**
   Italian manufacturing SMEs may attempt to interview or hire introduced candidates without paying the placement fee unless legal terms are signed prior to candidate scorecard unmasking.

## 2. Master Board Conditions Checklist

- [ ] **Contractual Shield:** Require every hiring partner to sign a binding 1-page Representation & Placement Agreement (standard 12-month candidate protection clause) before unmasking full candidate CVs and scorecard identities.
- [ ] **Cash Buffer & Milestone Payments:** Model cash runway carefully; pilot a 30% contract-signing fee with a 70% 90-day balance, or secure a minimum €10,000 personal/overdraft buffer before scaling past 3 active placements.
- [ ] **Candidate Value Proposition ("Fast-Track Pass"):** Pitch the assessment to students as the *"Plant Director Fast-Track"*: skipping HR screening and ATS black holes directly into a 20-minute final interview with the Operations Director.
- [ ] **Anti-Cheat Hardening:** Randomize simulation variables per session (lead times, holding costs, consumption rates) and disable clipboard paste in the browser interface.
- [ ] **Human Soft-Skills Gate:** The founder must conduct a mandatory 10-minute video pre-screen for all top scorers to verify communication skills, presence, and motivation before client presentation.
- [ ] **Defensible Simulation Library:** Expand beyond the V-104 valve scenario into 3-4 specialized manufacturing archetypes (automotive, packaging machinery, chemical process).

## 3. The Strongest Version of SkillBridge

The board sees SkillBridge not as another generic recruitment agency or a passive test-selling SaaS, but as **The Specialized Talent Clearinghouse for Italian Industrial Operations**. By mastering a singular, high-stakes pain point—verifying whether an engineering graduate can actually calculate stockouts, balance expediting costs, and keep an assembly line running—SkillBridge creates an unassailable wedge between the Plant Director and conventional HR. The winning playbook is clear: secure exclusive candidate distribution through direct student club partnerships at PoliMi and PoliTo, protect the placement fee with strict representation agreements, and deliver 3 pre-vetted, simulation-verified candidates who can speak the language of the plant floor on day one.
