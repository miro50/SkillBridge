# Executive Summary — SkillBridge

**SkillBridge is a performance-vetted recruitment clearinghouse that delivers ready-to-interview graduate Management Engineers for Supply Chain and Operations roles in Italian manufacturing companies.** Instead of screening buzzword CVs, SkillBridge validates candidate problem-solving on a realistic 25-minute assembly line breakdown simulation, backed by a 100% conditional placement fee payable only after 90 days of successful probation.

## 1. The Verdict & Core Financials: PROFITABLE
- **Contribution Margin:** **94%** ($3,940 contribution per placed candidate at $4,200 fee).
- **Accounting Break-Even:** **1 placement per month** (against an operational capacity of 6 placements/mo for a solo founder).
- **Year 1 Operating Profit:** **$105,500** on $147,000 of gross recruitment revenue (72% net margin).
- **Buyer Demand Validation:** **57% baseline buy rate** (100 simulated industrial buyers) surging to **75%** on the Grand Slam offer re-test.

## 2. Where the Board and the Buyer Panel Aligned vs. Disagreed
- **Alignment:** Both the Board and the Buyer Panel recognized that traditional agency CV filtering is broken for plant operations, and that the 90-day conditional guarantee completely eliminates buyer hiring risk.
- **Divergence:** The board worried about candidate cheating via AI and clients bypassing the placement fee. The buyer panel was less worried about cheating (readily convinced by the anti-cheat telemetry bonus) and more worried about corporate HR vendor policies and whether young engineers would leave the factory for Milan consulting firms after 6 months (neutralized by the 180-day retention warranty).

## 3. The Existential Risk & Solution
- **The Risk:** **Cash flow starvation from the 90-day realization cycle.** While accounting profit is high, invoices are collected 120 days after sourcing starts.
- **The Mitigation:** Maintain a minimum **€10,000 cash/overdraft cushion**, or test a **30% start fee / 70% 90-day split** to collect $1,260 upfront per placement.

## 4. What the Founder Needs to Start
- **Cash:** **$9,900 total initial liquidity** ($1,800 direct startup spend + $8,100 working capital reserve for Months 1–3).
- **Validation Milestone:** The **Live 5-Client Outreach Smoke Test** (securing 2 interview commitments from Plant Directors before writing non-essential software).
