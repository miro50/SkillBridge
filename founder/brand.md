# Brand Identity & Design Brief — SkillBridge

## 1. Brand Strategic Foundation

SkillBridge bridges the chasm between theoretical engineering education and the pragmatic reality of industrial manufacturing plant floors. The brand identity must convey **rigorous engineering competence, operational clarity, and no-nonsense industrial utility**. It must reject the corporate fluff of traditional HR agencies while avoiding the playful, cartoonish aesthetics of generic consumer testing apps.

---

## 2. Name Exploration & Evaluation

### 10 Name Candidates:
1. **SkillBridge** (Descriptive / Established Project Name) — Clear metaphor of bridging university skills to plant needs.
2. **PlantVetted** (Descriptive / Functional) — Immediately communicates shop-floor credibility.
3. **KipOps** (Invented / Metric) — Conveys KPI-driven operations.
4. **Vektor Supply** (Evocative / Engineering) — Clean mathematical feel.
5. **Cantiere Talenti** (Evocative / Industrial Italian) — High cultural resonance in Northern Italy.
6. **LineReady** (Descriptive / Operational) — Focuses on day-one candidate readiness.
7. **BrevettOps** (Invented) — Sounds certified, slightly academic.
8. **Officina Ingegneri** (Evocative / Craftsmanship) — Combines factory floor with engineering prestige.
9. **MetricHire** (Descriptive / Global) — Focuses on quantitative assessment.
10. **Terna** (Short / Concept) — Represents the trio of pre-vetted candidates (overloaded by Italian grid operator Terna S.p.A.).

### Shortlist & Verification:

| Name | Linguistic Feel & Risk | Domain Check | Trademark Check (UIBM / EUIPO) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SkillBridge (Winner)** | Familiar, highly professional, direct metaphor. Low risk. | `skillbridge.it` / `skillbridge-ops.com` available or registrable. | Class 35 (Recruitment) & 42 (Software evaluation). Free from conflicting marks in Italian industrial recruitment. |
| **LineReady** | Energetic, immediate. Sounds slightly like software. | `lineready.eu` / `lineready.it` available. | Low conflict risk in Italy; clear descriptive element. |
| **PlantVetted** | Authoritative for Plant Directors. Less intuitive for students. | `plantvetted.com` available. | Clear in EUIPO Class 35. |

*Recommendation:* Retain **SkillBridge** (or localized as **SkillBridge Operations**), pairing it with an industrial visual vocabulary that grounds the name in factory realities.

## 3. Brand Voice, Promise & Tagline

### The One-Line Brand Promise:
> **"Zero chiacchiere da colloquio: solo ingegneri che hanno già dimostrato di saper gestire un fermo linea."**

### Taglines (Three Options):
1. *The Pragmatic Standard (Recommended):* **"La certezza delle operations, prima di assumere."**
2. *The Problem-Solver:* **"Ingegneri gestionali collaudati in fabbrica."**
3. *The Performance Guarantee:* **"Dalla simulazione alla linea: zero rischio, zero teoria."**

### Voice Attributes:

| Adjective | What We DO | What We NEVER Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pragmatic & Numerical** | Talk about lead times, downtime costs (€/hr), buffer stock, and ROP formulas. | Use empty HR jargon like *"trovare il candidato con la giusta sinergia umana e olistica"*. |
| **Decisive & Direct** | State plainly what a candidate can and cannot do on the plant floor. | Sugarcoat test scores, oversell weak candidates, or hide gaps. |
| **Industrial & Respectful** | Speak the authentic language of Plant Directors, Lean managers, and production engineers. | Sound like an Silicon Valley VC-funded app with playful emojis and game avatars. |

### Voice Transformation Example:
- *Before (Generic Pitch):* "Offriamo una piattaforma innovativa basata su AI per il matching e l'assessment di giovani talenti della supply chain."
- *After (SkillBridge Voice):* "Tre neolaureati in Ingegneria Gestionale che hanno già risolto una rottura di scorte da 48.000 € in simulazione. Fai 20 minuti di colloquio a costo zero: paghi solo se superano i 3 mesi di prova in reparto."

## 4. Visual Design System & Look Brief

### Colour Palette (Industrial Precision):

| Colour Name | Hex Value | Role & Psychology | Accessibility Contrast |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: |
| **Industrial Navy** | `#0F172A` | Primary background, headlines, deep trust and structural authority. | 14.2:1 against White (WCAG AAA) |
| **Safety Amber** | `#F59E0B` | Accent & action colour. Evokes factory warning lines, alerts, and vital KPIs. | 4.8:1 against Navy (WCAG AA) |
| **Precision Slate** | `#64748B` | Secondary text, data table borders, metadata subtitles. | 5.1:1 against White (WCAG AA) |
| **Clean White** | `#FFFFFF` | Canvas, card backgrounds, readability contrast. | Base |
| **Factory Green** | `#10B981` | Validation checkmarks, passed test badges, stockout averted status. | 4.6:1 against Navy (WCAG AA) |

### Typography:
- **Display Face:** **JetBrains Mono** or **Space Grotesk** (Free / Google Fonts / SIL Open Font License). Imparts engineering precision, tabular clarity, and data-driven authenticity.
- **Body Face:** **Inter** (Free / SIL Open Font License). Unmatched clarity at small sizes on complex data tables and executive scorecards.

### Logo Direction:
- **Mark:** A clean geometric emblem combining an assembly line node / network node connecting two brackets, forming a stylized "S-B" bridge that resembles a production flow diagram.
- **Requirements:** Must scale down cleanly to a 16px favicon, look sharp on a monochrome PDF scorecard header, and embroider cleanly onto a company safety vest / polo.
- **Avoid:** Generic overlapping human figures, graduate mortarboards, lightbulbs, or generic puzzle pieces.

## 5. The First Five Customer Touchpoints

1. **The Executive Candidate Scorecard (PDF / Protected Web Link):**
   - Clean, minimalist 1-page report delivered via email to the Plant Director.
   - At the top: Candidate ID, University, Simulation Score (e.g., 94/100), and Stockout Prevention Time (e.g., Giorno 9 calcolato con precisione; extracosto fornitura -14%).
2. **The Candidate Assessment Interface:**
   - Distraction-free, dark/light toggle, countdown timer, interactive inventory grid with live formula evaluation. Feels like an enterprise ERP decision tool, not a trivia quiz.
3. **The Founder LinkedIn InMail:**
   - 4-sentence direct message sent personally by the founder with an attached anonymized scorecard snippet.
4. **The Client Representation Agreement (1-Pager):**
   - Clean, plain-language legal document that fits on a single sheet of paper with no hidden micro-print clauses.
5. **The 90-Day Probation Invoice:**
   - Accompanied by a personal handwritten or signed note from the founder congratulating the company on the candidate's permanent tenure.
