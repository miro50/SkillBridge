# CFO Source Register & Cost Documentation — SkillBridge

Every number used in `founder/numbers.json` traces to an audited real-world input or documented estimate:

## 1. Revenue & Pricing
- **Base Placement Fee (€4,200):** Based on 14% of €30,000 starting gross annual salary (RAL) for junior Management Engineers / Supply Chain Specialists in Lombardy and Emilia-Romagna (benchmark from Page Personnel Salary Guide 2026 & Almalaurea Graduate Employment Survey). Confirmed within the Van Westendorp acceptable price corridor (€2,503 – €5,250).

## 2. Variable Costs per Placement (€260 Total)
- **Candidate Academic & Identity Verification (€60):** Official diploma check with university registries and reference check phone calls. (Public university registrar request fees & admin time).
- **LLM Grader & Cloud Runner Execution (€40):** Anthropic / OpenAI API structured output tokens for evaluation scoring + temporary server compute for browser simulation sessions (<$5 per session, estimating 8 candidate sessions to yield 1 placed candidate = ~$40).
- **Contracting, Legal & Invoicing Admin (€60):** Electronic signature fee (DocuSign/Signwell API at €2/envelope) plus billing and collections administration.
- **Warranty Reserve (€100):** Provision for the 180-day free replacement guarantee. Assuming a 5% historic probation replacement rate on a €2,000 internal delivery cost = €100 expected cost per placed unit.

## 3. Fixed Monthly Costs (€2,700/month Total)
- **SaaS Infrastructure (€150/mo):** Supabase Pro plan ($25), Vercel Pro ($20), Resend transactional email ($20), domain & monitoring ($20), misc cloud SaaS. (Published vendor pricing).
- **Outreach & Prospecting Tools (€150/mo):** LinkedIn Sales Navigator Core subscription (€79.99/mo + VAT) plus Apollo.io / email warmup tool (€49/mo). (Published pricing).
- **Founder Living Stipend / Coworking (€2,200/mo):** Frugal founder base draw to cover living expenses and dedicated desk at an industrial incubator/coworking space in Milan/Bologna.
- **Accounting, Tax & Compliance (€200/mo):** Italian *commercialista* retainer for quarterly VAT filings, SDI electronic invoicing, and municipal compliance. (Standard Italian freelance accounting quote).

## 4. Startup Spend (€1,800 One-Off)
- **Legal Contracts & Representation Agreements (€1,000):** Specialized Italian labor/commercial lawyer quote to draft the binding 1-page Client Representation Agreement with anti-circumvention protection.
- **Domain, Branding & Security (€300):** 2-year `.it` and `.com` domain registration, SSL certificates, business email accounts on Google Workspace.
- **University Campus Partnerships & Materials (€500):** Physical flyers, roll-up banner, and student engineering association sponsorship fees at PoliMi and PoliTo career days.
