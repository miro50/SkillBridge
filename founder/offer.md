## 3. The Complete Offer Stack

- **The Core Product:** **3 Ready-to-Interview Operations Specialists** verified on the V-104 Assembly Line Disruption Simulation, delivered in 7 days with a 1-page executive decision scorecard.
- **Bonus 1 — Anti-Cheat & Proof Dossier (Value €600, Cost €10):** Full calculation steps, formula auditing, and browser integrity logs proving the candidate derived every lead time and downtime cost independently.
- **Bonus 2 — 180-Day Retention Warranty (Value €1,500, Cost €0 unless claimed):** If the candidate resigns or is let go within the first 6 months, SkillBridge supplies a replacement candidate at zero cost.
- **Bonus 3 — Founder Plant-Readiness Debrief (Value €400, Cost €50):** 10-minute briefing call with the Plant Director breaking down candidate strengths, communication traits, and specific onboarding suggestions.
- **The Guarantee:** 100% Zero-Risk Probation Guarantee. The invoice is issued at 90 days. If the candidate fails probation or is deemed unsuitable, the fee is cancelled completely with zero debt.
- **Urgency & Scarcity:** Limited to 5 partner plants in Northern Italy for Q4 2026 at the introductory pilot rate of **11% RAL (€3,300)** before reverting to standard 14% (€4,200).
