## 4. Ranked Buyer Complaints from Public Evidence

### Theme 1: Recruiter Ignorance of Technical/Plant Reality (Direct Agencies)
- **Complaint:** Traditional headhunters don't understand operations, ERP systems, or production constraints. They match keywords like "Lean" or "SAP" from CVs, sending candidates who crumble when asked basic production questions.
- **Evidence:** Feedback on industrial recruitment forums and agency reviews: *"I recruiter non capiscono la differenza tra un addetto logistica e un production planner con competenze MRP; mandano profili commerciali riciclati"* (Recruiter forums / Reddit Italy).

### Theme 2: Inflexible Annual Subscriptions for Low Hiring Volumes (Assessment SaaS)
- **Complaint:** Tools like TestGorilla or iMocha force manufacturing SMEs to sign €1,500–€4,800 annual contracts with expiring credits. A plant manager hiring only 1–2 specialists a year cannot justify an ongoing software subscription.
- **Evidence:** G2 & Trustpilot reviews: *"Mandatory annual lock-in; we had sporadic hiring and lost our credits"* ([G2 Review](https://www.g2.com/products/testgorilla/reviews), [Willo Video Analysis](https://www.willo.video)).

### Theme 3: Massive Friction & Drop-Off from Take-Home Case Studies (Substitutes)
- **Complaint:** When plant managers send out custom 4-hour take-home Excel assignments, top candidates refuse to do them, ghost the process, or have friends/ChatGPT complete the file. The plant manager then wastes hours grading flawed submissions.
- **Evidence:** Candidate forums on Reddit: *"Aziende che ti chiedono progetti Excel non retribuiti da 10 ore prima ancora del primo colloquio vengono scartate all'istante"*.

## 5. The Market Gap

**The Gap:** A specialized recruitment partner that delivers **ready-to-interview candidates whose practical problem-solving has already been verified in a 25-minute simulation**, backed by a **zero-risk 90-day guarantee**.

- **Traditional agencies** deliver unvetted candidates and charge 15–20% on day 1.
- **Testing software vendors** sell software to HR, but deliver zero candidates and demand annual subscriptions.
- **Internal HR** wastes 45 days and leaves the Plant Director drowning in unqualified CVs.

SkillBridge bridges this chasm: the Plant Director gets 3 pre-vetted candidates who proved they can solve an inventory disruption, with zero software to purchase, paying only if the hire stays past 90 days.

## 6. The Threat: Who Could Copy Fastest?

1. **Boutique Technical Recruitment Firms (e.g., specialised Milan recruitment boutiques):**
   - *How:* They already have candidate pipelines and client contracts. If they adopt interactive testing tools or partner with an assessment API, they could offer simulated scorecards within 2-3 months.
   - *Defense:* Speed to build exclusive university campus pipelines (PoliMi/PoliTo student associations) and hyper-realistic scenario libraries that generalists lack the engineering expertise to craft.
2. **Pre-employment Testing Platforms (TestGorilla):**
   - *How:* TestGorilla could launch an agency/marketplace wing.
   - *Defense:* Highly unlikely—testing vendors operate on SaaS gross margins (80%+) and avoid labor-intensive contingency recruitment workflows in regional industrial clusters.
