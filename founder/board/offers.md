# Offers lens
Score: 8/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Hair-on-fire problem & high willingness to pay:** A factory downtime costs €1,200/hour; a single bad inventory decision can tie up tens of thousands of Euros in working capital. Industrial SMEs already routinely pay 15–20% of RAL (€4,500–€6,000) to recruitment agencies for keyword-matched CVs that fail on the job.
- **Superior Value Equation:** 
  - *Dream outcome:* A junior operations engineer who can read MRP anomalies and resolve bottlenecks on day one.
  - *Perceived likelihood of achievement:* Dramatically elevated by tangible 25-minute simulation scores and a 100% 90-day probation guarantee.
  - *Time delay:* Compressed from the industry standard 45–60 days down to under 10 days (3 pre-vetted candidates ready to interview).
  - *Effort & sacrifice:* Reduced to near zero for the Plant Director—no reviewing 40 CVs, no manual take-home grading.
- **Irresistible risk-reversal offer:** "Zero upfront fee, 3 vetted candidates with operational scorecards, pay 12-15% only after 90 days of successful probation." It makes saying no irrational for a plant manager struggling with open positions.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Cash flow starvation from delayed realization:** Invoicing at 90 days post-hire means the founder gets paid 120–150 days after candidate introduction (sourcing + interviewing + 3-month probation). Without upfront cash or working capital, rapid placement growth will deplete founder reserves.
2. **Disintermediation / bypass risk:** Industrial SMEs are notorious for circumventing recruitment fees, claiming the candidate was already in their database, or delaying contracts. Without signed terms of business before candidate reveal, placements will be stolen.
3. **Candidate candidate-side drop-off:** Top engineering graduates often hold multiple formal job offers from consulting or multinational corporations; if asked to take an unbranded 25-minute test with no guarantee of employment, they may abandon the funnel.

## The questions I would ask the founder
1. How will you prevent plant directors from directly contacting candidates and bypassing your placement fee without heavy legal overhead?
2. If you don't get paid until day 90 of employment, what is your survival runway if your first 3 placements take 4 months to pay?
3. What is the explicit incentive for a high-GPA PoliMi or PoliTo graduate to spend 25 minutes on your test before a recruiter even calls them?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- [ ] Enforce a strict, legally binding 1-page Candidate Representation Agreement signed by the hiring client *before* unmasking candidate identities and scorecards.
- [ ] Test a staged payment structure (e.g., 30% upon signed employment contract, 70% at 90 days probation) or maintain at least €10,000 cash buffer to survive the 120-day revenue lag.
- [ ] Package the candidate side not as a "test", but as a "Fast-Track Career Pass" directly to the Plant Director, bypassing HR screening.
