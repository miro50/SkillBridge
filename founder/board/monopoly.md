# Monopoly lens
Score: 7/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Sharp, defensible wedge in a concentrated niche:** By deliberately rejecting 7 generic verticals (dev, finance, PM, sales) and owning "Supply Chain & Operations Specialists for Italian manufacturing", SkillBridge builds specialized credibility that generalist agencies (Michael Page, Adecco, Hays) and generic testing platforms (TestGorilla) cannot match.
- **The Secret:** The hiring manager (Plant Director / Operations Manager) is frustrated by their internal HR department and external headhunters because neither understands plant mechanics, ERP lead times, or bottleneck calculations. Offering a domain-specific simulation directly to the line manager exploits this organizational fracture.
- **Proprietary evaluation data:** If SkillBridge tests hundreds of operations engineers, it creates an authoritative, proprietary performance benchmark for junior operational talent in Italy.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Low technological defensibility against agency incumbents:** A boutique agency model has low barriers to entry. If established recruiting giants with thousands of enterprise client relationships simply license an assessment vendor or build interactive case studies, they can crowd out SkillBridge.
2. **Distribution / two-sided chicken-and-egg bottleneck:** To attract top industrial clients, you need verified candidate inventory; to attract top graduates, you need confirmed job openings with top manufacturers. If outbound prospecting to Plant Directors stalls, the engine stops.
3. **Scaling ceiling of boutique recruitment:** The recruitment wedge is service-heavy. While margins per placement are high, scaling beyond 5 placements a month requires adding human recruiters, diluting the software leverage and monopoly potential.

## The questions I would ask the founder
1. What proprietary asset is being built that makes the business 10x harder to duplicate 18 months from now?
2. What stops TestGorilla or Gi Group from bundling an operations module once they see you succeeding?
3. How quickly can this evolve from manual founder placement into a software-driven proprietary marketplace?
