# Product lens
Score: 8/10 | Vote: FUND

## What is strong
- **Radical, disciplined focus:** Saying NO to 7 other verticals and focusing exclusively on Supply Chain & Operations Specialist roles. This protects the team from feature bloat and creates an unmistakable product identity.
- **Crafted, realistic evaluation scenario:** The V-104 valve disruption scenario replicates the visceral reality of a plant floor (stockout date, assembly line downtime cost of €1,200/hr, air-freight expedited lot economics). It immediately differentiates SkillBridge from toy multiple-choice trivia quizzes.
- **Fast, respectful user experience:** 25 minutes continuous in-browser test with zero software installation, magic link login, auto-save every 15s, and a 1-page executive scorecard delivered cleanly to the hiring manager.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Assessment cheating / LLM exploitation:** Candidates could easily paste the problem into Claude or ChatGPT to compute the stockout and draft the justification, undermining the integrity of the scorecard.
2. **Scorecard complexity / cognitive overload for buyers:** If the 1-page scorecard is too academic or cluttered with algorithm metrics rather than clear, hiring-relevant signals ("Can this candidate manage my shift? Yes/No"), busy Plant Managers won't use it.
3. **Seam failure between the simulation and the hiring outcome:** If a candidate achieves a top score on the simulation but lacks basic soft skills, communication, or culture fit during the interview, the Plant Director will blame the SkillBridge tool and churn.

## The questions I would ask the founder
1. How do you protect the 25-minute simulation against LLM paste-assistance without turning the test into an intrusive proctored spyware nightmare?
2. What does the scorecard look like, and can a Plant Director understand it in 15 seconds?
3. How do you vet communication skills and work ethic before putting candidates in front of the client?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- [ ] Implement robust anti-paste and randomized variable injection (changing unit costs, consumption rates, and lead times per session) so static prompt answers cannot be shared or solved trivially by generic LLMs.
- [ ] Conduct a 10-minute structured human video screening for each candidate in the top tier before presenting them to clients.
- [ ] Design the scorecard so the primary takeaway is visible in under 10 seconds: Math Accuracy, Operational Logic, and Recommended Action.
