# Pricing Strategy & Model — SkillBridge

## 1. The Recommended Price & Justification

- **Price:** **€4,200 per successful placement** (equivalent to 14% of a standard €30,000 entry RAL for junior Supply Chain Specialists / Operations Engineers).
- **Position in the Acceptable Range:**
  - Van Westendorp analysis on 100 industrial buyers established an acceptable pricing corridor of **€2,503 (PMC) to €5,250 (PME)**.
  - The Indifference Price Point (IPP) is **€3,961**, and the Median "getting expensive" threshold is **€4,600**.
  - At €4,200, SkillBridge sits directly in the sweet spot: sufficiently high to convey premium, rigorous engineering vetting (avoiding the <€2,500 "resume mill" stigma) while sitting below the €5,250 barrier where buyer resistance hardens.
- **Competitor Benchmark:**
  - Traditional agencies (Page Personnel, Hays, Wyser) charge 15%–20% (€4,500 – €6,000) payable on contract signing with weak replacement guarantees.
  - SkillBridge undercuts traditional agencies slightly on percentage (14% vs 18%) while completely crushing them on risk reversal (100% conditional at 90 days).
- **Unit Margin:**
  - Variable cost per placement: €200 (LLM grader tokens, academic credential check, contracting).
  - Contribution margin per placement: **€4,000 (95.2%)**.
  - At planned volume (3 placements/month), net operational profit margin is **74%**.

## 3. Opening Pilot Offer (Launch Wave)

- **The Offer:** *"First-Hire Risk-Free Pilot"*: 
  - Fee discounted from 14% to **11% of RAL (€3,300)** for the first 5 industrial partner companies in Lombardy/Emilia-Romagna.
  - Zero upfront deposit.
  - Free 20-minute introductory interview with 3 candidates.
  - Invoiced strictly at 90 days after successful completion of probation.
  - In exchange, the Plant Director agrees to provide a written recommendation / case study quote upon successful probation.
- **Expiration:** Valid strictly for the first 5 signed pilot agreements or within 45 days of outreach launch.

---

## 4. What to Test with Real Buyers (A/B Test Plan)

1. **Test A: Flat 14% at 90 Days** (Standard guarantee pitch: *"Pay €4,200 only after 90 days of work"*).
2. **Test B: Staged Split (30% at signed contract / 70% at 90 days)** at a discounted total of **€3,900**:
   - Tests whether buyers prefer a lower total price in exchange for small skin in the game, which would radically improve founder working capital and shorten the 120-day cash realization cycle.

## 5. Panel Price Objections from Simulated Buyers

1. *"Il 14% della RAL (4.200 €) è troppo alto per un ragazzo uscito dall'università senza esperienza precedente."* (P005, P012)
   - *Marketing Counter:* Frame the cost against the €1,200/hour cost of an assembly line shutdown caused by an unqualified hire who miscalculates reorder points.
2. *"Preferisco fargli fare un colloquio tecnico dal vivo con il mio capo reparto prima di impegnarmi su cifre da agenzia."* (P025)
   - *Marketing Counter:* The 20-minute interview and scorecard review are 100% free with zero commitment; you only pay if you hire them and they survive 90 days on the plant floor.
3. *"La garanzia a 90 giorni è ottima, ma che succede se il candidato se ne va al quarto mese?"* (P010, P017)
   - *Marketing Counter:* Free candidate replacement within 180 days if the employee leaves voluntarily.
